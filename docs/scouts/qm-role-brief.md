## QM Role Responsibilities (Scouts Australia policy)

### Primary responsibilities

**1. Inventory Management**
- Maintain accurate inventory of all group equipment
- Per item: name, condition, purchase price, estimated replacement value, storage location, purchase date
- Preferred format: spreadsheet (Google Sheets suitable)

**2. Equipment Checkout**
- Issue equipment to members/leaders with documentation
- Check condition on return, record findings
- Report lost/damaged items

**3. Condition Reporting**
- Regular (quarterly) summary to Group Support Committee
- Repair recommendations with cost estimates
- Replacement procurement recommendations

**4. Insurance Valuation**
- Keep the group's equipment insurance values current
- Scouts Australia national policy covers group property, but QM must document the values

### Secondary duties (events)
- Equipment logistics for events (40th anniversary: tents, tables, cooking gear etc.)
- Equipment needs assessment in advance, transport coordination
- Post-event inventory check

## Mandatory compliance requirements

### 1. Queensland Blue Card (Working with Children Check)

| Detail | Info |
|--------|------|
| Who needs it | All adult volunteers working with young people |
| Fee | Free for volunteers |
| Processing time | 7-21 business days |
| Validity | 3 years |
| Link | https://www.qld.gov.au/law/laws-regulated-industries-and-accountability/queensland-laws-and-regulations/regulated-industries-and-licensing/blue-card/applications/apply |

**Process:**
1. Apply online via the QLD Government Blue Card portal
2. Scouts QLD links your Blue Card account number to the organisation (via Group Leader)
3. Until approved: cannot work with young people outside supervised settings
4. Renewal: system sends notification 3 months in advance

**Exception:** QM role is primarily admin/equipment management - but as a Group Support Committee member attending events, Blue Card is MANDATORY.

### 2. Scout Fundamentals Training (mandatory online)

- Available on the Scouts Australia Terrain platform
- Login: my.scouts.com.au
- Time required: approx. 2-3 hours (self-paced)
- Content: safeguarding, child protection, Scout values, health & safety

### 3. Scouts Australia membership registration

- Terrain platform: https://terrain.scouts.com.au
- Membership fee: via Scouts QLD (Group Leader handles)

## Insurance & Liability

### Scouts Australia coverage
- **Public Liability:** $20M cover for all approved Scouts activities
- **Personal Accident:** extends to volunteers in case of injury
- **Property insurance:** group property covered (based on QM inventory)

### QM responsibilities from insurance perspective
- Up-to-date equipment inventory (required for insurance claims)
- Estimated replacement values updated annually
- If equipment is lost/damaged: document (date, circumstances, estimated value)

### Important limitation
- QM is not responsible for event insurance cover (that is Group Leader / Scouts QLD territory)
- QM is equipment management, not risk management
